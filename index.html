I am a staff scientist in cancer genomics in the Division of Genome Analysis Platform Development at National Cancer Center Research Institute. My research focuses on cancer (epi)genome analysis using long-read sequencing, with the goal of understanding the full spectrum of genomic alterations in cancer, including those hidden in regions that are difficult to resolve with short reads. Please feel free to contact me by e-mail.

Research interests

  • Haplotype-resolved cancer genomics: personalized reference genomes built from long reads, enabling allele-aware analysis of somatic mutations, structural variants, and epigenetic changes (PRCGAP).
  • Structural variants and phasing: detection and characterization of complex rearrangements in lung cancer and other tumors using nanopore sequencing.
  • Long-read epigenomics: whole-genome methylation profiling with enzymatic base conversion and nanopore sequencing (nanoEM).
  • Complex genomic regions: centromeres and other repetitive regions in human diversity and cancer.
